Drag a person onto a station they're skilled for (home or extra skill), in either shift lane. Green border = allowed, red = not skilled for it (give them the skill on the Staff & Skills tab first). Their old home is kept as a skill. Ctrl + drag a CNC operator onto another CNC to have them run both (they stay on their own machine; the second runs at a reduced rate). Click × on the ghost to stop. Coverage dot: ● at/above ideal crew   ● below ideal   ● nobody   dashed chip = more people than the machine can use.
